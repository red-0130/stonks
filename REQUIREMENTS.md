# Stock Analysis App

## Features
- User can select a stock to analyze by ticker symbol
- shows current price of the stock
- rates the stock based on the following:
    - P/E Ratio less than 20
    - ROIC greater than 15%
    - Debt/Equity Ratio is less than 1
    - EPS is greater than 10% CAGR
    - ROE is greater than 15%
    - EBIT Margin is greater than 10%
    - Gross Margin is greater than 40%
- provides a summary of the rating
- provides a brief summary of the market sentiment
- provide a technical analysis
- provide trade strategies with entry and exit (also show strategy success percentage)
