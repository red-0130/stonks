# Swing Trade Strategy
## Steps
1. Top-Down Analysis (Weekly)
    - Analyse the Weekly timeframe to determine general trend direction using price structure
    - Mark imbalances and key supply and demand zones to find your POIs for entries and targets

2. Top-Down Analysis (Daily)
    - Refine zones and imbalances on the 1D timeframe for more precision
    - Identify any refined 1D opportunities inside of the larget 1W trend.

3. Confirmation and Execution (1H-4H)
    - Use the 1H-4H timeframes to locate your 'standard confirmation' inside 1D zones.
    - Execute positions form 1H-4H refined zones following confirmations

4. Trade Management (1H-4H)
    - Decide upon a trailing, partial or breakeven only system
    - Follow your management plan by drawing stops higher after each validated low/high
    - Take partials (if decided) as per the pre-defined plan & don’t deviate

5. Trade Exists
    - Exit as per take profit levels & don’t panic close too early (limiting returns)
    - Consider the dynamic RR of holding vs closing winning positions close to target
    - Aim for a minimum 3:1 Return on Risk to balance variance & maintain positive PnL
    - Never move stops & always work with a contingency exit plan if trades go bad

## Market/Price Structure
### What is market structure?

Market structure is the theory of ‘trends’ that we use to determine which direction a market is
moving in. It’s one of the first steps in preparing setups & understanding markets.

### Reading Trends

We determine the direction of trends by reading high & low structure.
    - Market creating higher highs & higher lows = UPTREND (BUY)
    - Market creating lower highs & lower lows = DOWNTREND (SELL)

!NOTE: Markets will also trade in phases of misdirection (sideways price action) called consolidation.

When a market is clearly uptrending we look only for BUY (long) trades through the significant
structure. When it’s downtrending we only look to SELL (short).

During consolidation phases we simply DO NOT TRADE as there is no clear direction.

### Impulses vs Corrections

Each trending move is made up of a combination of impulse & corrective moves.
    - The IMPULSE is the pro-trend move creating new highs or lows.
    - The CORRECTION is the counter-trend move creating a ‘pullback’

When trading with the trend we want to catch the IMPULSE moves as this is the real trend
direction. Corrections provide opportunities for enter trades using other concepts. The goal
is to buy on corrections to ride markets through the next impulsive moves.

### Changes in Trend Direction

Markets don’t trend one way forever. There will be reversals/shifts. A reversal can be
identified when impulses reverse & a market changes from forming higher highs & higher lows
into lower lows & lower highs (bearish reversal) or from lower lows & lower highs into higher
lows & higher highs (bullish reversal).

### Swing Structure vs Sub-Structure

Structure can be defined in 2 formats ~ sub vs swing structure.
    - Swing-structure is the significant structural move on the larger timeframe.
    - Sub-structure is the smaller trends inside of trends on lower timeframes.

The theory here suggests we can trade with the larger SWING structure, BUT we will also have
smaller trends inside of trends that we can actually use for additional opportunities. Inside of
each of the pullback moves in the swing structure uptrend will be a standalone substructure
downtrend that can provide new trades for us on top of the original idea.

When we understand swing structure & substructure we can become ‘dynamic’ traders and
benefit from trade opportunities on the HTFs through swing structure, as well as trade
opportunities on the MTF/LTFs using sub-structures smaller ‘trends within trends’.

### Timescale Relativity

Market structure ~ as with all price action concepts ~ is timeframe relative & ‘fractal’ meaning
the same patterns form on every single timeframe whether the 1 month or 1 minute. For this
reason you can trade using the concepts of market structure regardless if you’re a hyper low
timeframe scalp trader, day trader, swing trader or long term position trader.

### Market Structure Utility

Structure is one of many concepts we use. It’s the core concept in determining trending
direction which is generally going to be the first step in finding any trade opportunities.
Alone, structure doesn’t provide us with what we need to create entry models, execute &
manage positions, but it does give us the directional bias’ and confirmations that we use to
formulate entries.
